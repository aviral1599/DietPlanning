# Nutrient data sources and the NIN 1989 cross-check

_Last reviewed: 24 Nov 2026._

## Which source the app uses

| What | Source | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Ingredient nutrition (per 100 g) | **IFCT 2017**, Indian Food Composition Tables (NIN-ICMR), for 170 of 227 ingredients. The rest come from documented label/USDA values, now partly replaced by NIN 1989 values (see below). | `src/lib/foodbank/ingredients.ts`, generated by `scripts/foodbank/gen_ingredients.py` |
| Dish nutrition | Always **computed** from ingredient weights. Nothing is typed in by hand, except the strained-juice fibre on two drinks. | `src/lib/foodbank/bank.ts` |
| Exchanges | The clinic's **11-group Food Exchange List**. Dish exchanges are computed from the same per-exchange values (`EXCHANGE_UNITS`). | `src/lib/exchange-data.ts` |
| RDA comparison | **ICMR-NIN 2020** RDAs | `src/lib/nutrition.ts` |
| Reference only | **Nutritive Value of Indian Foods** (NVIF), NIN-ICMR 1989 ed., 1993 reprint | `src/lib/nvif/` and the Food Bank → "NIN 1989 reference" tab |

Decision (Nov 2026): IFCT 2017 stays the main source, because it is NIN's newer replacement for NVIF and uses modern methods. NVIF is used to **verify** the data and to **fill gaps** where we had only foreign or label values. The clinic exchange list and the ICMR-NIN 2020 RDAs stay.

## Why the two books can't be swapped directly

| | NVIF 1989 | IFCT 2017 (app) |
|---|---|---|
| Carbohydrate | By difference: 100 − moisture − protein − fat − ash − crude fibre. **Includes most dietary fibre and organic acids.** | Available carbohydrate (starch + sugars) |
| Fibre | **Crude fibre**, typically 20–40% of dietary fibre | Total dietary fibre |
| Energy | 4 / 4 / 9 kcal per g (P / C / F) | From analysed components (fibre ≈ 2 kcal/g) |

Rajma is an example. NVIF gives 60.6 g carbs and 4.8 g fibre; IFCT gives 48.6 g carbs and 16.6 g fibre. Total carbohydrate is similar (≈ 65 g), but the split is very different. Swapping in NVIF values would cut plan fibre by about 70% and inflate carbs and glycaemic load. To compare fairly, the cross-check compares **total** carbohydrate: IFCT carbs + fibre against NVIF carbs + crude fibre.

## Book data in the repo

`scripts/foodbank/nvif1989/` contains the hand transcription of the book's common-foods tables. They were read from page images, because the scan's OCR is unusable. Values are exactly as printed.

- Table 1, proximate principles: all 451 common foods (moisture, protein, fat, minerals, crude fibre, carbs, kcal, Ca, P, Fe).
- Table 2, vitamins: 325 rows (carotene, B1, B2, niacin, B6, folate free/total, vitamin C, choline).
- Table 3, minerals and trace elements: 215 rows (Mg, Na, K, Cu, Mn, Mo, Zn, Cr, S, Cl). Variety rows such as "dhal" vs "whole" are kept.
- `mapping.py` links 169 food bank ingredients to book foods. 147 are exact matches; 22 are marked approximate (related form or variety).
- The exchange table (Table 2, p. 41) and the 1989 RDAs (Appendix I, p. 94) are in `src/lib/nvif/reference.ts`.
- Not transcribed: the "less familiar foods" tables, amino acids, fatty acids, B12 and the non-nutrient tables.

Rebuild the data file with `python3 scripts/foodbank/nvif1989/gen_nvif.py`. Re-run the cross-check with `npm run foodbank:nvif` (add `-- --all` for every flagged item).

### Misprints in the book (kept as printed, ignored in judgements)

- Cluster beans (#146) printed as 16 kcal; the macros give ≈ 60 kcal.
- Fan-palm jaggery (#444) components sum to 110 g.
- Buffalo milk (#419) printed as 117 kcal; the macros give ≈ 96.
- Rajmah (#43): fibre given in brackets, components sum to 105 g.
- Fermented toddy (#448) energy doesn't match its macros.
- Wheat (#20): free folate 142 µg is greater than total folate (36.6).
- Orange (#283): potassium 9.3 mg.
- Piyal seeds (#209): copper printed "086".
- Book Table 2 gives the meat exchange serving as "75" with only 7.5 g protein.

## Results (run of 24 Nov 2026)

### 1. Food bank sync

All **447 dishes** recompute exactly from the **227 ingredients**, with no stale or hand-typed numbers. The only exceptions are the deliberate strained-juice fibre on ash gourd juice and amla juice. Dish exchanges now read their divisors straight from `EXCHANGE_UNITS`, so dishes and the exchange list can't drift apart:
- cereal 15 g carbs;
- pulse and meat 7 g protein;
- milk 8 g protein;
- fruit and root vegetables 10 g carbs;
- sugar 5 g;
- fat and nuts 5 g fat;
- other vegetables 100 g.

### 2. Ingredients vs the book (147 exact matches; flagged where ≥ 30% and above a minimum size)

| Field | Flagged | Main reason |
|---|---|---|
| Protein | 2 | Dried apricot, water chestnut (the book's is probably a different variety). |
| Fat | 7 | Egg (book 13.3 g vs IFCT 9.2 g), fish fat varies with season (sardine, pomfret), lean mutton cut, avocado variety. |
| Total carbs | 8 | Fruit varieties (grapes, strawberry), tapioca moisture, raw banana. |
| Calcium | 25 | Mostly **fish: the book analysed small fish with bones** (rohu 650 mg vs fillet 39 mg); also dry coconut. |
| Iron | 44 | Old iron assays picked up contamination (e.g. curry leaves 0.93 vs 8.67 mg, watermelon 7.9 mg); greens vary widely. |
| Potassium | 34 | The 1989 flame-photometry values are erratic (e.g. orange 9.3 mg, beetroot 43 mg). |
| Sodium | 11 | Same; prawn sodium differs because IFCT measures sea prawns. |
| Vitamin C | 21 | Vitamin C is lost in storage and varies by variety (amla 252 vs 600 mg, cabbage 33 vs 124 mg). |
| Folate | 16 | The 1989 microbiological assay vs modern methods. |

Conclusion: **macros agree for about 95% of matched ingredients**. The micronutrient differences come from the 1989 methods, so IFCT 2017 values are kept.

### 3. Dishes recomputed with the book's values

The median dish energy change is **+3%**. 73 dishes shift more than 10% in kcal and 25 shift more than 15% in protein. These are almost all fruits, amla, coconut water and herbal waters, because the book's carbohydrate counts fibre and fruit acids as 4 kcal/g. Eggs shift because of the book's higher egg fat, and gawar because of the misprint. None of this points to errors in the food bank.

### 4. Clinic exchange list vs the book

This compares the portion that gives one exchange, against the nutrient that defines each group.

| Group | Items checked | Median difference | Items ≥ 15% |
|---|---|---|---|
| Milk (8 g protein) | 9 | 0% | buffalo milk +17% |
| Meat (7 g protein) | 12 | −2% | chicken breast +18% |
| Pulses (7 g protein) | 13 | +4% | cow pea, moth, soybean +17–18% |
| Cereals (15 g carbs) | 15 | +3% | — |
| Fats / nuts (5 g fat) | 10 | 0–3% | — |
| Fruits / roots (10 g carbs) | — | not comparable | The book's carbs include fibre, so its portions come out smaller. |

The book's own six-list system uses different serving sizes, for example milk ½ cup (100 ml) = 3.5 g protein / 4 g fat / 4 g carbs / 65 kcal, and cereal 25 g raw = 85 kcal. Its nutrient-per-gram figures are consistent with the clinic list, so the clinic list is kept.

### 5. RDAs

The book's 1989 adult RDAs:
- **Sedentary man:** 2425 kcal, 60 g protein, 20 g visible fat, 28 mg iron.
- **Sedentary woman:** 1875 kcal, 50 g protein, 30 mg iron.
- **Both:** 400 mg calcium.

ICMR-NIN 2020, which the app uses, revised energy down (2110 / 1660 kcal sedentary) and updated most micronutrients. The 1989 values are kept in `reference.ts` for history only.

## Gaps filled from the book

These ingredients previously relied on foreign or label data, and the book has the same Indian food. The book's protein, fat, Ca and Fe are used (plus vitamin C and folate where given). Available carbs = book carbs + crude fibre − dietary fibre. Energy is recalculated as 4P + 9F + 4C + 2·fibre. Na and K stay from the earlier source unless the book gives them.

| Ingredient | Book food | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Watermelon seeds (magaz) | #213 | P 28.3→34.1, F 47.4→52.6, carbs 15.3→4.5, fibre 0→0.8 (crude, a lower bound), Ca 54→100 |
| Chironji | #209 Piyal seeds | Added Na 10.2 and K 436, which were missing |
| Honey | #440 | 304→319 kcal, Fe 0.4→0.7 |
| Sabudana | #446 Sago | 358→349 kcal, Ca 20→10 |
| Brown bread | #26 | P 12.4→8.8, F 3.5→1.4, Ca 125→18 (Indian vs US enriched bread) |
| White bread | #27 | F 3.3→0.7, Ca 60→11 |
| Sugar | #439 | Added Ca 12, Fe 0.16 (energy kept at the exchange list's 20 kcal / 5 g) |
| Mixed seeds | computed | Recomputed after the watermelon seed update |

Brand-label items stay on their labels, because they describe the product actually used: Amul toned, double-toned and full-cream milk, curd, processed cheese, and fresh cream.
